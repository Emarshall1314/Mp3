# M6 demo: Sync

Sync keeps tag edits, artwork, favourites, play counts and the Trash the same on your devices,
through a place they all reach: a **folder** (kept in step by Syncthing or Nextcloud's desktop
client, or on a drive you move between machines) or a **server** (WebDAV such as Nextcloud, or
S3-compatible storage such as Backblaze B2). There's no Cadenza server and no account.

You need two devices with Cadenza, or one machine and the second-device helper described at the
end. Each device needs its own copy of the music, or the "share songs" option (section 6).

## 1. Favourites and the Trash (one device)
1. Right-click a song: **Add to Favourites**. The heart in the player bar fills when it plays;
   click the heart to remove it again.
2. Right-click a song: **Move to Trash**. It disappears; the message has **Undo**.
3. **Trash** (sidebar): the song is listed with the days left (30). **Restore** puts the file back
   where it was, with its play count. **Delete Now** deletes the file at once.

## 2. Start syncing
1. Make an empty folder that your devices share (a Syncthing folder, for example), outside your
   music folders.
2. **Sync** (sidebar) → **Choose Folder…** and pick it. Cadenza says it has no library yet.
   Leave **Encrypt** unticked for now and press **Start Syncing**.
3. Give this device a name (e.g. "Laptop").
4. On the second device, choose the same folder: it says the folder holds a library shared by one
   device. Press **Join**. After a minute (or **Sync Now** on both), **Devices** lists both.

## 3. Edits, favourites and listens
1. On the laptop, change a song's title in Get Info… and favourite another song; play a third
   to the end.
2. Sync Now on the laptop, then on the other device (or wait a minute). Expected there: the new
   title, in the song's file too (Get Info… shows it); the favourite; the play count went up.
3. Sync again on both: nothing is counted twice.

## 4. Conflicts
1. Change the same song's artist on both devices before either syncs.
2. Sync both, then the first again. Expected: both show the same artist (the later edit), and
   the sidebar's **Sync** shows a count and the page has a **Conflicts** entry naming both values, devices and times.
3. Choose **Use “…”** for the other value. Both devices change to it and the conflict goes.

## 5. The Trash, everywhere
1. Move a song to the Trash on one device; sync both. On the other device the song's file is in
   its Trash too.
2. **Restore** it on the first device; sync both. It's back on both.

## 6. Sharing songs between devices
1. On the laptop, tick **Copy this device’s songs to where you sync**. Sync: the songs are
   copied into the folder (the sidebar shows progress; **Pause** stops it and it carries on later).
2. On a device without those songs, tick **Copy songs other devices put there**.
   Sync: they're copied into its first music folder and appear in the library, with the
   favourites and play counts they have elsewhere.

## 7. Encryption
1. Make another empty folder and choose it in **Sync** → **Change…**. Tick **Encrypt it with a
   passphrase**, type a passphrase twice (8 characters or more) and **Start Syncing**. Sync shows
   **Encrypted**.
2. Look inside the folder: file names are random-looking and nothing in it is readable.
3. On the other device choose the same folder: it asks for the passphrase. A wrong one is
   refused; the right one joins.

## 8. A server (WebDAV or S3)
1. **Sync** → **Use a Server…**: choose WebDAV and enter your Nextcloud address (Nextcloud's
   Settings → WebDAV shows it; add a folder name at the end, e.g. `/Cadenza`), user name and an
   app password. Or choose S3 and enter the endpoint, bucket, keys and a folder in the bucket.
2. **Connect**. Encryption works as for folders.
3. Optionally set a **bandwidth limit** for song transfers.

## Second device on one machine
`cargo run -p cadenza-api --example sync_device -- <data dir> <music dir> <sync folder> <name>
["title=<song title>=<new title>"] ["artist=<song title>=<new artist>"]` runs a second library in
its own data folder: it scans, makes the edits given, and syncs. Set `CADENZA_PASSPHRASE` for an
encrypted folder.
