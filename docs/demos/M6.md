# M6 demo: Sync over Wi-Fi

Sync keeps tag edits, artwork, favourites, play counts and the Trash the same on your devices
while they're on the same Wi-Fi with Cadenza open. Devices find each other on the network, pair
with a code, and copy each other's changes directly, encrypted. There's no server, no account and
nothing goes over the internet. Changes made while apart catch up the next time they meet.

You need two devices with Cadenza on the same network, or one machine and the second-device
helper described at the end. Each device needs its own copy of the music, or the "copy songs"
options (section 7).

## 1. Favourites and the Trash (one device)
1. Right-click a song: **Add to Favourites**. The heart in the player bar fills when it plays;
   click the heart to remove it again.
2. Right-click a song: **Move to Trash**. It disappears; the message has **Undo**.
3. **Trash** (sidebar): the song is listed with the days left (30). **Restore** puts the file back
   where it was, with its play count. **Delete Now** deletes the file at once.

## 2. Pair two devices
1. On the first device: **Sync** (sidebar; Settings → Sync on the phone). Give it a name
   ("Laptop") and press **Start Syncing**. It shows a **pairing code** like `5JPV-F5VG-TSNB`.
2. On the second device, open **Sync**: **Join a Device** lists "Laptop" within a few seconds.
   Press **Join…**, type the code (any case, dashes optional) and **Join**.
3. Both now show the code, and **Devices** lists both with a green dot when the other is
   reachable. A wrong code is refused.
4. Not listed? Some networks hide devices from each other (guest Wi-Fi, "client isolation" on
   some routers). Use **Enter its address** with the address shown on the first device's Sync
   page. If that fails too, the network blocks devices talking to each other: sync can't work
   on it.

## 3. Edits, favourites and listens
1. On the laptop, change a song's title in Get Info… and favourite another song; play a third
   to the end.
2. Within a few seconds the other device has them (it's told there's news), or press **Sync Now**.
   Expected there: the new title, in the song's file too (Get Info… shows it); the favourite; the
   play count went up.
3. Sync again on both: nothing is counted twice.

## 4. Apart, then together
1. Close Cadenza on one device (or take the phone off the Wi-Fi). Edit something on each.
2. Bring them back together: both get the other's edits within a minute (or Sync Now).

## 5. Conflicts
1. With one device away, change the same song's artist on both.
2. Bring them together and sync. Expected: both show the same artist (the later edit), and the
   Sync page has a **Conflicts** entry naming both values, devices and times.
3. Choose **Use “…”** for the other value. Both devices change to it and the conflict goes.

## 6. The Trash, everywhere, and Continue Listening
1. Move a song to the Trash on one device; the other's copy goes to its Trash too. **Restore** it:
   it's back on both.
2. Play an album on the laptop and pause part-way through a song. On the other device, **Sync**
   shows **Continue Listening** with the song, the laptop's name and where it got to. **Continue
   Here** plays the same queue from the same song and spot.

## 7. Copying songs between devices
1. On the laptop, tick **Let my other devices copy songs from this one**.
2. On a device without those songs, tick **Copy songs from my other devices**. Sync: they're
   copied straight from the laptop into its first music folder (the page shows progress; **Pause**
   stops it) and appear in the library with their favourites and play counts.
3. With the laptop away, the page says how many songs are waiting for a device that has them.

## 8. Stop syncing
**Stop Syncing** leaves the library: this device's music and edits stay as they are, and the
others carry on without it. To sync again, join one of them with its code.

## Second device on one machine
`cargo run -p cadenza-api --example sync_device -- <data dir> <music dir> 127.0.0.1:<port>
<pairing code> <name> ["title=<song title>=<new title>"] ["artist=<song title>=<new artist>"]`
runs a second library in its own data folder: it joins the app (the port and code are on the
app's Sync page), scans, makes the edits given and syncs, then stays reachable for 30 seconds so
the app copies its changes. `"play=<song title>"` plays from that song and pauses, for Continue
Listening.
