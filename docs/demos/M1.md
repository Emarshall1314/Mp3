# M1 demo: Foundation

Prerequisites are the same as M0 (`sudo tools/setup-linux.sh`, Rust, Node 22, pnpm 10,
`pnpm install`). Use your own music folder, or the fixture library from `cargo xtask fixtures`
(`fixtures-out/library`).

1. **Start with an empty library.** Run `pnpm dev`.
   - On first run you see **Add your music** with one button.
   - Click it and choose a folder.
   - "Building your library…" shows progress, then albums appear as they are read.
   - A built app can be started already pointed at a folder:
     `cadenza-app --add-folder ~/Music`.
2. **Browse.**
   - **Albums**:
     - Drag the size slider; the grid reflows.
     - Change Sort by; the choice is remembered. **Recently Added** in the sidebar shows the newest
       300 albums.
     - Albums without an artist or title sort last.
   - **Album page**:
     - Open an album to see the artwork, artist link, genre · year · quality badge, and discs.
     - Same-named releases in other folders appear under **Other Versions**.
     - In the fixture library, open Night Drive by Gamma.
   - **Songs**:
     - Click column headers to sort; click again to reverse.
     - Scroll quickly; rows are virtualised and stay smooth at 200k tracks.
   - **Artists**: select one, then press Play or Shuffle.
3. **Search.**
   - Type in the sidebar search field. Results update as you type: albums, artists and songs.
   - Try the advanced syntax: `format:flac year:>2000`, `bits:24`, `artist:"alpha band"`, `quality:dsd`.
   - Press **Save Search**. The saved search appears in the sidebar.
4. **Play.**
   - Double-click a song, or use an album's Play button.
   - The player bar shows the title, artist, artwork and quality badge; hover the badge for the
     exact format.
   - Drag the scrubber to seek. Pause/resume, next/previous, volume, shuffle and repeat all work.
   - Open the queue (the list button at the right of the player bar). Remove items, or use Clear.
   - Right-click a song or album for Play Next, Play Later, Go to Album and Go to Artist.
5. **Gapless.**
   - Play `fixtures-out/gapless/` albums (sine sweeps split across tracks) in MP3, AAC, Opus,
     Vorbis and FLAC.
   - There are no clicks or gaps at track changes.
   - `cargo test -p cadenza-decode` asserts this to the sample.
6. **Watcher.**
   - With the app open, copy an album into the music folder. It appears within a few seconds.
   - Delete it and it disappears.
   - Move a folder: play counts are kept because the identity is the audio hash, not the path.
7. **Music Folders.**
   - Shows the folders, totals and the last scan report.
   - Files that could not be read are listed with the reason; the fixture `corrupt/` group shows
     several.
   - Removing a folder never touches the files.
8. **Checks and performance.**
   - `cargo xtask ci` runs all lints, fixture generation and all tests.
   - `cargo xtask bench` builds a 200k-track synthetic library and prints:
     - rescan time (budget: seconds)
     - search times (budget: 50 ms)
     - deep paging time
     - open time

Not in M1 (not shown in the UI):
- exclusive/bit-perfect output and the device picker
- ReplayGain, EQ
- tag editing
- playlists
- settings
- media keys
