# M5 demo: Customisation

Everything here is in **Settings** (Ctrl+,) unless it says otherwise. Every change shows at once
and is saved by itself; nothing needs restarting.

## 1. Themes
1. Under **Appearance**, pick Light, then Dark. Under **Theme**, choose "When light" and pick
   **Paper**, then "When dark" and pick **Midnight**. Switch Appearance between Light and Dark:
   each uses its own theme. Auto follows the desktop.
2. Try every built-in theme. Expected: text is easy to read in all eight.
3. With Midnight chosen, press **Duplicate and Edit**. A panel opens on the right.
   - Change Background to a different colour: the whole app changes as you type.
   - Set Text to `#333333`: a warning says text contrast is below WCAG AA.
   - Type `#12` into a colour: "Not a colour" appears and Save is greyed out. Put the colours
     back, name the theme and **Save Theme**. It appears among the theme cards, chosen.
4. **Export** the theme, then **Delete** it, then **Import…** the exported file. It comes back.
5. Optional: open the exported file in a text editor and add `"color-bg": "url(https://x)"`.
   Import refuses it: themes never load anything from the web.

## 2. Accent, background and panels
1. **Accent Colour**: click the blue preset. Links, buttons and the play bar's icons turn blue.
   Click the colour square and choose any colour.
2. Play an album with colourful artwork, then choose **From Artwork**: the accent takes the
   cover's most colourful part. Play another album: it follows.
3. **Background**: Gradient shows the theme's gradient behind everything; Artwork Tint colours
   it from the playing cover.
4. **Panels**: Frosted Glass blurs what's behind the sidebar and player bar (scroll an album
   grid under the player bar to see it). **Check the blur on your desktop**: it couldn't be seen
   on the test display. Move the Blur and Transparency sliders.
5. **Liquid Glass**: on Linux the panels get bright rims and a sheen. On the phone they also
   bend the content behind them at their edges, like thick glass.
6. Turn on **Reduce transparency**: panels become solid.

## 3. Shape and text
1. Density Compact, then Spacious: the song list's rows get shorter, then taller, and still
   line up when you scroll.
2. Artwork size Large: album pages and Now Playing show bigger covers.
3. Font: try Serif, then "Other installed font…" and type a font you have (e.g. `DejaVu Sans`).
4. Text size 120 %, corner rounding 0 % (square), then **Reset Look**.

## 4. Layout
1. Drag the sidebar's right edge: it resizes. Double-click it: icons only. Double-click again.
2. **Settings → Sidebar**: untick Recently Added and move Songs to the top. The sidebar follows.
3. **Player Bar**: Top, then Large. Open the output menu: it opens downwards. Untick Shuffle and
   Mini player; move Queue above Volume. Then Bottom and Standard again.
4. **Views**: in Albums, switch to the list view with the toggle beside Sort by. Leave and come
   back: it stays a list. Do the same for Artists (grid of artists).
5. **Songs**: drag the Album heading to before Artist; drag the right edge of the Artist heading
   to widen it. In Settings → Song List Columns, add Album Artist and Format.
6. **Home**: hide Most Played and move Recently Added first.
7. **Now Playing**: choose Lyrics First, play a song with lyrics, open Now Playing: it opens
   on the lyrics with a smaller cover. Minimal: just the cover and controls.

## 5. The panel
1. Press Ctrl+U: the panel opens on the right with Queue, Lyrics, Info and Signal tabs. Info
   shows the playing song's credits and file; Signal shows the signal path.
2. Use the panel's move button: Dock Left, Dock at Bottom, then Float. Drag the floating panel
   by its tabs' row; resize it from its bottom-right corner. Drag the edge of the docked panel to
   resize it.

## 6. Layout presets
1. Press the layout button at the top right of any page: Listening, Tagging, Minimal.
   - Tagging: the songs-first sidebar and tagging columns.
   - Minimal: icons-only sidebar, three columns, few controls.
2. Press Ctrl+Shift+L repeatedly: it steps through the presets, naming each.
3. Arrange something you like, then **Settings → Layout Presets**, name it and **Save Current
   Layout**. It appears in the menu.

## 7. Shortcuts
1. **Keyboard Shortcuts**: click Next track's keys, press Alt+N. Alt+N now skips; Ctrl+→ no
   longer does.
2. Give Previous track the keys Alt+N: Next track loses them, and a note says so.
3. Give Shuffle a shortcut (it has none by default) and try it. Then **Reset All Shortcuts**.

## 8. Custom CSS (desktop)
1. In **Custom CSS**, enter:
   ```css
   :root { --color-accent: #ff9f0a; }
   .sidebar h3 { text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.1em; }
   ```
   and **Apply**: the accent turns orange and the sidebar's headings are in capitals.
2. Enter `body { display: none; }` and Apply. The window goes blank. Press **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S**:
   everything comes back, and Custom CSS says it's off until Cadenza restarts. Clear it.
3. Safe mode from a terminal: `cadenza --safe-mode`.

## What to report
- Anything hard to read in any theme or combination.
- Whether the blur and liquid glass look right on your desktop and on the phone.
- Any layout that can't be undone without Reset Layout.
