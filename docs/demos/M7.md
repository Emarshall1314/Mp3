# M7 demo: Mobile (so far)

The phone layout is used whenever the window is narrower than 700 px, so most of this can be
tried on the desktop by making the window narrow. On the phone it's what you always see (after
the next Android build).

## 1. Settings that stay

1. Settings: choose a different theme and accent colour. Close the app completely (on Android,
   swipe it away from recent apps) and open it again. Expected: the same theme and accent.
2. If the app says some settings couldn't be read, note the message: the old file is kept next
   to the settings as `settings.json.unreadable`.

## 2. Tabs

1. Make the window narrow (or open the app on the phone). Tabs appear along the bottom: Home,
   Library, Search, Settings.
2. **Library**: Recently Added, Artists, Albums, Songs, Saved Searches, and under More the rest
   of the app. Open an album: the back button returns to Library.
3. **Search**: the box is ready to type in; results appear as you type.
4. Settings → Layout → **Phone**: tick Albums. An Albums tab appears. Reorder with the arrows.
   Library can't be removed; a sixth tab can't be ticked.

## 3. Mini player and Now Playing

1. Play a song. The mini player sits above the tabs: artwork, title, play/pause, next, and a
   progress line.
2. Settings → Layout → Phone → **Mini player**: try Floating, Bar and Minimal.
3. Tap the mini player (or swipe it up): Now Playing opens. Drag the handle at the top down to
   close it.
4. In Now Playing, open lyrics or the queue: they take the screen, with the controls below.

## 4. Lists

1. Songs: each row shows the title with the artist beneath and the time. Tap a row to play it.
2. On a touch screen, press and hold a song: its menu opens (the phone vibrates briefly).

## 5. Liquid glass

1. Settings → Appearance → Panels: **Liquid Glass**.
2. The tabs float in a glass capsule with Search as a round button beside it; the player is a
   capsule above. The page shows through and scrolls underneath.
3. Tap another tab: the glass lens behind the current tab slides there, stretching as it goes.
   Press on the lens and drag along the bar: it follows your finger and picks the tab you let go
   on.
4. Scroll a page down: the bar shrinks to the current tab and the player moves in beside it.
   Scroll up (or tap the small tab) to open it again.
5. Press and hold the player: it lights up from your finger and swells slightly.
6. On the phone, tilt it slowly: the bright edges of the glass move with the light.
7. On the phone, look at the edges of the bar and player over artwork: lines bend as they pass
   under the rim, with a faint colour fringe.
8. Settings → Appearance → Reduce Motion stops the stretching, swelling and tilt light; Reduce
   Transparency turns the glass off.

## 6. Polish

1. Albums: the grid fills the screen in two columns with even margins; the size slider's small
   end gives three. Tap **Sort by**: a glass menu with a tick, not the system picker.
2. With Background set to Artwork, skip a few songs: the colours fade across slowly.
3. Open Now Playing: play/pause sits in a glass button; the lyrics and queue buttons are below
   the controls and open in a rounded glass card.
4. Scroll quickly through Albums and Songs: is it smooth?

## 7. Motion and swipes

1. Skip songs: the artwork changes without flashing the default picture.
2. Scroll a page down and up: the bar narrows to a circle and the player glides beside it,
   smoothly, and back.
3. Change tabs: the lens reaches across and settles, never outside the bar.
4. Settings → any switch (Light / Dark…): the highlight slides and stretches.
5. Open an album from Albums, play a song, go to Home, open Now Playing and swipe right: the
   album. Swipe right again: the Albums grid.
6. On any page, swipe right to go back; let go early and the page springs back.

## 8. Android back

1. Open an album, then Now Playing. Back (button or gesture from the edge): Now Playing closes.
   Back again: the page before. Keep going: Home, then the app goes to the background and the
   music keeps playing. Reopen it: where you were.

## 9. Rediscover

1. Home: **Rediscover** is at the top. With some listening history you'll see an Album of the
   Day, a few mixes and (after a fortnight) albums you've never played.
2. Tap a mix: its page lists the songs; Play or Shuffle. Swiping right from Now Playing comes
   back to the mix.
3. Tomorrow, the picks are different; today they stay the same however often you open Home.
4. Settings → Layout → Home: Rediscover can be moved or hidden like the other shelves.

## 10. Third round

1. Now Playing: drag along the progress bar; it thickens, follows your finger and stays where
   you let go. Tap a spot to jump there.
2. Songs → Group by → Album: headings above each album's songs.
3. Albums: − and + change the size (4 to 1 columns); pinch the grid to do the same.
4. Scroll so the dock shrinks: the player stays clear of the Search circle, smoothly.
5. Open an album and swipe right slowly: the Albums grid is already there underneath. From the
   Albums (or Home) page, swipe right: Now Playing comes in from the left.
6. Artists (with more than 12): touch the letters down the right edge and slide; let go on C.

## 11. Playlists

1. Press and hold an album (or right-click on the desktop): Add to Playlist… → New Playlist…,
   name it. Do the same with another album, choosing the playlist this time.
2. Library → Playlists: the playlist, with its cover. Open it: Play, Shuffle, rename (pencil),
   delete (bin; Undo in the message).
3. Drag a song by its grip to a new place; tap ✕ to remove one.
4. Home → a Rediscover mix → Save as Playlist.

## 12. Fourth round

1. Scroll a long list quickly and stop: the dock pieces lean with the scroll and spring back.
   Small back-and-forth scrolls no longer make the dock flip.
2. Tap Search: the bubble grows into the search field; the bar becomes a circle (tap it to go
   back).
3. Albums or Songs (a big library), or Artists: slide down the letters; the letter shows large
   at the top of the screen and the list jumps.
4. Settings: sections with icons; open Appearance, Layout; switches and sliders.
5. Turn the phone sideways: the same glass dock (centred), Now Playing side by side.
6. Linux, KDE: Settings → Appearance → See-through window, with KWin's Blur or Force Blur on.

## What to report

- On the phone: whether the look survives closing the app (most important).
- Anything cut off, overlapping or too small to tap at your phone's size.
- Whether long-press triggers too easily while scrolling, or too slowly.
- Whether the glass is smooth while scrolling on the phone, and how it compares with iOS.
- Whether Rediscover's picks feel right, and which mixes you'd add.
