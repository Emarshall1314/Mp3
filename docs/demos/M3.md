# M3 demo: Design polish

Run this on the Linux desktop where Cadenza is installed (`pnpm dev`, or a built package). Use the
fixture library (`cargo xtask fixtures`, then add `fixtures-out/library` as a music folder) or your
own music. Part 5 needs a real desktop session.

## 1. Home
1. Start Cadenza. It opens on **Home**.
   - On a fresh library, only Recently Added shows, with a note that played albums will appear.
2. Play an album through, or skip through its tracks to the end, then go back to Home.
   - **Recently Played** and **Most Played** now show it.
   - **See All** on Recently Added opens the full Recently Added grid.

## 2. Artist and album pages
1. Go to Artists and choose an artist you've played. Expected:
   - The artist's artwork and name, with Play and Shuffle.
   - **Top Songs**, most played first, with play counts. Hidden for artists you haven't played.
   - Releases grouped by type: Albums, EPs, Singles, Live, Compilations, Soundtracks. This comes
     from the release-type tag that MusicBrainz Picard writes; untagged releases show as Albums.
   - **Appears On**: other artists' albums where this artist is credited on a track.
2. Open one of their albums. Below the tracklist, **More by \<artist\>** lists their other albums.
   Compilations don't show it.

## 3. Now Playing and lyrics
1. Play `Lyric Album` from the fixtures, or any song with lyrics. Click the artwork in the player
   bar. Expected:
   - Now Playing fills the window, over a slow gradient in the artwork's colours.
   - Pause: the artwork springs smaller. Play: it grows back.
2. The lyrics panel is open:
   - The current line is highlighted and stays centred.
   - Click any line: playback jumps to it.
   - A song without lyrics says where Cadenza looks: a `.lrc` file with the same name next to it,
     or lyrics embedded in the file.
3. Switch the panel to the queue with the button at the top right. Esc closes Now Playing.

## 4. Appearance and motion
1. Open **Settings** (sidebar, or Ctrl+,). Choose **Dark**, then **Light**, then **Auto**.
   - The whole app switches at once, mini player included.
   - Auto follows your desktop's dark/light setting, and changes when the desktop does.
2. Tick **Reduce transparency**. The sidebar, player bar, queue and menus become solid.
3. Tick **Reduce motion**. Views, the queue and menus appear without animating, and the Now Playing
   background stops drifting.
   - With both switches off, turning on your desktop's own reduce-motion setting has the same
     effect.
4. Quit and reopen Cadenza. The appearance choices are kept.

## 5. Desktop integration (real desktop session)
1. **Media keys**: press Play/Pause, Next and Previous on the keyboard. Cadenza responds.
2. **Media widget**: your desktop's media controls (GNOME's notification area, KDE's media
   player applet) show the title, artist, album and artwork, and can control playback.
3. **Tray**: the Cadenza icon appears in the status area. This needs KDE, or GNOME with the
   AppIndicator extension. Its menu shows the current track, Play/Pause, Next, Previous, Mini
   Player, Show Cadenza and Quit.
4. **Mini player**: Ctrl+M, the player bar button, or the tray. A small always-on-top window
   with artwork and transport. Drag it by its body; the expand button brings back the main window.

## 6. Keyboard only
Put the mouse away:
1. Tab through the sidebar to Songs and press Enter.
2. Tab into the song list. Use ↑/↓, Page Up/Down, Home and End to move. Enter plays.
   - One more Tab leaves the list, however long it is.
3. Press Shift+F10 (or the Menu key) on a song. Arrow keys move through the actions, Enter runs
   one, and Esc closes the menu and returns to the song.
4. In Albums, arrow keys move between albums; Enter opens one.
5. Playback shortcuts:
   - Space: play/pause.
   - Ctrl+→/←: next/previous track.
   - Shift+→/←: ±10 s.
   - Ctrl+↑/↓: volume.
   - Ctrl+L: Now Playing with lyrics.
   - Ctrl+U: the queue.

   Settings lists every shortcut.

## Automated checks
`cargo xtask ci` runs the parts that need no eyes:
- `core/library`: LRC parsing (repeated stamps, offsets, word stamps), lyric source order,
  artwork palettes, release-type sections, Top Songs, Appears On and the Home shelves.
- `core/api/tests/api.rs`: appearance saved across restarts.
- `ui/src/shortcuts.test.ts`: shortcuts, including Space leaving text fields and buttons alone.
